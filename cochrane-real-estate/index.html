Living in Cochrane

Cochrane is a town west of Calgary on Highway 1A, along the Bow River, with views toward the Rocky Mountains.

Buyers like Cochrane for its small-town feel, quick access to the mountains and a historic main street, while staying close to Calgary's northwest.

What to Check When Buying in Cochrane

Hills and slopes

Many lots are on hills. Check drainage, retaining walls and the Real Property Report.

Commute

Highway 1A and Cochrane traffic can be busy at peak times. Test the drive.

River areas

Near the Bow River, check flood maps and insurance options.

Property taxes

Cochrane sets its own tax rate. Compare the actual tax bill.
