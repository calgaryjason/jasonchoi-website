What Sellers Ask Me Most

What is my business worth?

I review sales, profit (SDE/EBITDA), lease terms, equipment and industry averages. Two businesses with the same sales can have very different values because of the lease.

Will my staff and customers find out?

No. I market the business without its name and share details only with buyers who sign an NDA and show proof of funds.

Asset sale or share sale?

Taxes and liabilities are different. I work with your accountant to find the best structure for you.

Should I offer seller financing (VTB)?

For businesses that banks do not like to finance, a VTB can be the key to closing. I show you how to structure it safely.

How does the landlord approval work?

I check the assignment terms early and prepare the buyer package so the landlord can approve quickly.

Calgary Area Guide

Calgary's 8 Real Estate Districts

The Calgary Real Estate Board (CREB®) divides the city into 8 districts. MLS® searches use these districts.

View CREB® Official Map →
CCCity Centre

Downtown, Beltline and inner-city condos. Walkable, close to work and amenities.

NNorth

Newer family communities north of the city, linked by Stoney Trail.

NWNorthwest

Established neighbourhoods near Nose Hill, the university and the mountains.

NENortheast

Good value, close to the airport, with C-Train access.

EEast

The most affordable area, with major shopping in East Hills.

SESoutheast

Family-friendly lake communities and Fish Creek Park.

SSouth

Mature and newer communities on both sides of Macleod Trail.

WWest

Sought-after communities near the mountains and top schools.

Not sure which area fits you? I can help you find the right area for your budget and lifestyle.

Who I Am

This website is run by Jason Choi, REALTOR® with First Place Realty (Alberta) and Maple Supreme Realty Inc. (British Columbia). I protect your personal information under Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What I Collect

I only collect what you choose to send me through the forms on this website. This can include your name, email, phone number, the property or business you are interested in, your budget and your message.

Why I Collect It

I use your information to answer your questions, send you listings you asked for, prepare valuations and provide real estate services. I do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Who Helps Me

Form messages are delivered to my email by Web3Forms. The website is hosted by Cloudflare, which may collect basic, anonymous visitor statistics. These services may store data outside Canada, including in the United States.

Sharing for a Transaction

If we work together on a transaction, I may share the needed information with my brokerage, lawyers, lenders, inspectors or the other party's REALTOR®, only as required to complete your transaction.

Emails and Listing Alerts

I only send listing alerts and market updates if you agree. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying "unsubscribe" or by contacting me.

How Long I Keep It

I keep your information only as long as needed for these purposes, or as long as the law or my brokerage requires.

Your Rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information at any time. Please contact me at CalgaryJason@gmail.com or (403) 399-8124.
