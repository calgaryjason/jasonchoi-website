Description

Available for sale is the iconic Trail Daily Times Building at 1163 Cedar Avenue, occupying a prominent location in the heart of downtown Trail, BC, with direct visibility from Highway 3B. A new torch-on roof and HVAC system were completed in July 2026 at a cost exceeding $130,000. Centrally positioned between Kootenay Savings Credit Union’s headquarters and the recently renovated Austin Engineering (BBA Consultants) building, the property is within a 1-minute walk of Ferraro Foods, Starbucks, Shoppers Drug Mart, and numerous downtown amenities. The building comprises ~8,969 SF across two floors and is currently 100% leased. Longstanding tenants include Steps Dance Centre, occupying the upper floor under a new 5-year net lease expiring in 2031, and Black Press Group (Trail Times), occupying the main floor with a lease expiring August 2027, providing investors with established income or owner-occupiers with potential to occupy for their own use. Trail benefits from a strong regional economy anchored by Teck’s Trail Operations, one of the world’s largest integrated zinc and lead smelting and refining complexes, generating over $2 billion in gross revenue in 2024.
