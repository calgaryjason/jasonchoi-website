Description

Offered for sale as shares C and D in the desirable Juniper Lodge, this ownership opportunity provides 26 weeks of annual usage determined by an established usage calendar, along with excellent potential to generate rental income. Featuring 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and comfortable accommodation for up to six guests, this well-appointed mountain condo offers a bright, open-concept layout with vaulted ceilings, a cozy electric fireplace, and access to a private balcony. The fully equipped kitchen features stainless steel appliances and ample space for preparing meals and entertaining after a day of mountain adventures. The spacious primary bedroom features a King bed, while the second bedroom offers two Twin beds and the added convenience of its own separate exterior entrance. This unique feature provides additional privacy, flexibility for guests, and attractive rental possibilities. Additional features include complimentary Wi-Fi, cable TV, and dedicated ski storage. Owners and guests also enjoy access to the shared amenities at Timberline Lodges, including a hot tub, heated outdoor pool, and BBQ facilities. Ideally located just a five-minute walk from the base of Fernie Alpine Resort, this property provides convenient access to skiing, snowboarding, mountain biking, hiking, dining, and year-round outdoor recreation.
