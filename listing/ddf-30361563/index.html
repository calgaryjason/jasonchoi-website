Welcome to this spacious top-floor condo in Juniper Lodge, ideally located within the sought-after Timberline Lodges complex at Fernie Alpine Resort. Offering 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and accommodation for up to six, this well-appointed mountain property features a bright, open-concept floor plan highlighted by vaulted ceilings, an electric fireplace, and access to a private balcony. The fully equipped kitchen features stainless steel appliances and ample space for preparing meals and entertaining. The property is approximately a five-minute walk from the base area at Fernie Alpine Resort, providing convenient access to skiing, snowboarding, dining, and year-round recreational activities. Whether used as a personal mountain retreat, vacation property, or rental investment, this well-located condo offers an attractive combination of space, flexibility, and resort amenities in one of Fernie’s premier alpine destinations.
