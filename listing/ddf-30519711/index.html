Exceptional industrial investment opportunity available only minutes from the city of Cold Lake. Located in a rural industrial subdivision, this 0.49 acre industrial lot allows a variety of uses and business configurations. The lot is fenced with chain-link security fencing, and a large gravel surfaced yard approximately 10,800 sq. ft. in size for parking and storage is situated at the front of the property. This versatile building has over 6300 sq. ft. of usable space, including over 500 sq. ft. of industrial office space. It features two shops on either end of the building, with overhead roll-up garage doors over 13 high and 15 wide. The shops are fully heated with overhead natural gas heaters and sit on a finished concrete pad. Between both shops are offices spaces, storage, and washrooms that could be configured to support one or more tenants/businesses. Each shop has access to an office, washroom, and storage area, including a potential painting/clean room in one of the shops. A hallway connects both halves and features a laundry area, plus a 1500 gal. cistern for water. The mezzanine is accessible from both sides, and the second level has a large open storage loft, another office, and a spacious staff break-room and kitchen area. The current lease has more than a couple years left and provides immediate rental income, making it a perfect fit for someone looking for industrial space in the long-term while benefiting from the supplemental income in the short-term. The property offers incredible flexibility for a variety of businesses, including both commercial and industrial uses with the opportunity for further specialization.
