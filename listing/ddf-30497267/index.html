Description

A remarkable 160-acre country estate overlooking the dramatic Donalda Badlands and Meeting Creek Coulee, where privacy, recreation and refined rural living come together in an unforgettable setting.Built in 2008, the beautiful bungalow is positioned to capture the views, with a walk-out basement, wraparound deck and sweeping vistas across the surrounding prairie and coulee landscape. Inside, vaulted ceilings with striking fir beams, hardwood and tile flooring, and generous natural light create a warm, sophisticated interior with timeless country character.Comfort has been thoughtfully considered throughout. The home features an efficient boiler heating system with in-floor heat on both the main level and basement, along with a wood-burning stove. The property is off-grid with solar power, supported by propane and a drilled well, offering an impressive level of independence while maintaining the comforts of a beautifully finished home. The basement is built with ICF construction, and the metal roof adds to the home’s enduring appeal. The outdoor spaces are every bit as exceptional. Gather with family and friends around the outdoor kitchen and pizza oven entertaining area, relax on the wraparound deck, or simply take in the peaceful views and extraordinary quiet of the countryside. Recreational features include a private five-hole golf course with artificial greens, a labyrinth, and a pickleball setup inside one of the large pole sheds. The land offers a beautiful combination of productive acreage and pasture, with approximately 77.92 acres assessed as agricultural land and roughly 74 acres of pasture. Mature trees, open grassland and natural contours contribute to the property’s picturesque, private setting.The property is also well equipped for agricultural or recreational use, with large pole sheds with concrete floors, three 2,500-bushel hopper bottom bins, a dugout, a greenhouse, an insulated garage and partial fencing along the south and west sides. This is a property that truly stands apart — ideal for someone seeking a luxury country residence, private recreational retreat, small farm or self-sufficient lifestyle in one of Central Alberta’s most striking landscapes.
