Description

A great opportunity to acquire New To Me Sports, a Calgary based sporting goods business with two active customer locations and a practical business model that fits today’s market. The main retail store is located at #122, 5334 72 Avenue SE, with a second location operating as the Bowness Pro Shop at the Bowness Community Association, 7904 43 Avenue NW.New To Me Sports has built its business around a simple and attractive concept for Calgary families, athletes, teams and recreational sports users. The business offers a wide range of new and used sports equipment, giving customers access to quality gear at different price points while also creating a convenient option to buy, sell, trade in or trade up.The business serves multiple sports and seasonal categories, including hockey, baseball, football, golf, lacrosse, racquet sports, skates, snow sports, soccer, apparel, accessories and more. This broad product mix helps the business appeal to parents with growing kids, adult recreational players, teams, coaches and customers looking for value on equipment they need right away.In addition to retail sales, New To Me Sports offers equipment related services that create repeat customer traffic and added value. These include skate sharpening, skate molding, skate rivets, hockey stick repair, baseball glove relacing and custom apparel options for teams, groups and individual customers. These services help separate the business from a basic retail store and give customers more reasons to return throughout the year.The Bowness Pro Shop adds another valuable customer touch point and gives the business additional exposure within a busy community sports environment. Having two locations allows the business to serve different parts of Calgary, strengthen its local presence and create more opportunities to connect with players, families, teams and associations.This may be an excellent fit for an owner operator, family buyer, entrepreneur or existing sporting goods operator looking to expand in the Calgary market. With two locations, a recognizable name, a practical buy, sell and trade model, service based revenue opportunities and room for further growth, New To Me Sports offers a unique entry point into a retail business connected to sport, families and the local community.Further details, financial information, lease information, equipment lists and transition details are available to qualified buyers.
