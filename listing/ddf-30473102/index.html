Build Your Dream Home at Whispering Greens! Discover the opportunity to build your ideal home on this spacious, fully serviced residential lot in Vulcan's desirable Whispering Greens community. Measuring approximately 52 feet wide by 230 feet deep, this vacant, grass-covered lot offers generous depth and backs onto the golf cart path, with the 18th green of the Vulcan Golf Course just beyond. Located within the Whispering Greens Homeowners Association, this predominantly 45+ community features architectural guidelines requiring a minimum 1,200 sq. ft. bungalow with a front attached garage and natural ("organic") fencing. There is no required timeline to begin construction, allowing you to purchase now and build when the time is right. Homeowners Association fees become payable once the property is developed. The Town of Vulcan offers a hospital, medical services, shopping, recreational facilities, and an 18-hole golf course, all conveniently located approximately one hour from Calgary or Lethbridge. Whether you're planning for retirement or simply looking to enjoy a quieter lifestyle, this property offers an excellent opportunity to build your next home in an established golf course community.
