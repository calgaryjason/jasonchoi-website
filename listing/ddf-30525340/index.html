Description

Rare opportunity to acquire a +/-12,903 SF industrial cross-dock warehouse on a +/-1.7-acre, R6-zoned site in downtown Cranbrook, BC. Located at 1505 4th Street North, the property offers a compelling combination of established industrial income and long-term high-density residential redevelopment potential. The site comprises two lots totalling over 74,000 SF and is improved with a functional truck terminal featuring 7 truck-level loading doors, 6 grade-level doors, clear heights ranging from 17'4"" to 26'9"", mezzanine office space, and an expansive yard accommodating large transport trucks and trailers. The building features three-phase 400-amp and 200-amp services and is leased to two long-standing tenants, ACE Courier (since 2012) and Black Press (since 2000), currently operating an Amazon depot, both on flexible month-to-month tenancies. R6 High-Density Multiple Family Residential zoning provides significant redevelopment potential, subject to City approvals, supported by ongoing residential intensification along the 4th Street North corridor. Strategically positioned minutes from Highway 3/95 and downtown amenities, this offering presents an opportunity for investors, industrial owner-users, and residential developers. (id:68043)
