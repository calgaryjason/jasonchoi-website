What Sellers Ask Me Most

What is my business worth?

I review sales, profit (SDE/EBITDA), lease terms, equipment and industry averages. Two businesses with the same sales can have very different values because of the lease.

Will my staff and customers find out?

No. I market the business without its name and share details only with buyers who sign an NDA and show proof of funds.

Asset sale or share sale?

Taxes and liabilities are different. I work with your accountant to find the best structure for you.

Should I offer seller financing (VTB)?

For businesses that banks do not like to finance, a VTB can be the key to closing. I show you how to structure it safely.

How does the landlord approval work?

I check the assignment terms early and prepare the buyer package so the landlord can approve quickly.

Calgary Buyer Checklist

8 Things to Check Before You Buy in Calgary

Calgary homes have local issues to check. I review these with you at every showing.

01Hail & Roof

Calgary gets frequent hail. Check the roof, siding, windows and past insurance claims. Impact-resistant (Class 4) shingles may help with insurance.

02Radon Gas

About 1 in 6 Calgary homes is above the radon guideline, and newer homes can be higher. A long-term test after you move in is recommended.

03Legal Basement Suite

A second kitchen does not make a suite legal. Check the City's Secondary Suite Registry. It affects rental income and insurance.

04RPR & Compliance

Under the standard Alberta contract, the seller provides a Real Property Report with City compliance. It shows decks, garages and fences.

05Older Plumbing

Homes from the 1980s–90s may have Poly-B or Kitec pipes. Check if they were replaced, because of leak risk and insurance.

06Flood Risk Areas

Near the Bow and Elbow rivers, check the flood maps. Past 2013 flood damage and insurance options also matter.

07Condo Documents

Review fees, the reserve fund study, meeting minutes and any special levies. Problems show up in the documents first.

08School Designation

Designated schools can change from street to street. Check with the CBE and Catholic board before you buy.
